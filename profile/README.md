## IslandOS

IslandOS (**IsOS**) connects a deployment graph, robot execution, cognition, and a browser workbench. It makes the path from observation to decision, admitted action, and outcome inspectable.

- **IsOS** owns verified packages, saved graph revisions, Apply, process supervision, and the IsosUI workbench.
- **EOS** owns device adapters, health, command admission, and physical execution.
- **EI** owns cognition stages and a local safety gate. It requests actions through EOS.

The graph connects these parts, while each core keeps its own authority. EOS remains the sole actuator route.

**Target design — not implemented.** We are working toward a body-aware Embodied Hardware Abstraction Layer (E-HAL), a Python intelligence runtime for tensor-based robotic planning and learning, and an open library of qualified nodes and skills. These are goals to build and measure, not claims about current behavior. The [public IslandOS repository](https://github.com/Island-Team/isos-aether) documents the supported implementation.

## Our groups

Island Team brings together **Agent** and **MachineLearning** for cognition and models; **Arm**, **Base**, and **Hardware** for physical systems; and **FrontBackend** for web experiences and services. The groups collaborate through explicit, versioned interfaces.

## What guides us

- Ground intelligence in sensing, action, and physical feedback.
- Keep graph, model, runtime, and hardware boundaries clear.
- Record failures and compare methods before calling a capability reliable.
- Admit physical actions through EOS, with predictable Stop behavior.
